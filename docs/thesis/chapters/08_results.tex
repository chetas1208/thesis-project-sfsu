\section{Legacy Multi-Model Benchmark}

Table~\ref{tab:legacy-llm} (Chapter~\ref{ch:baselines}) establishes RQ1--RQ2: among four completed open-weight models trained for three epochs under identical pipeline code, Mistral Small 3.1 24B achieved the highest exact span F1 (0.665), token F1 (0.730), and partial span F1 (0.798).
Parse failure rate was 0.028\% (1 of 3{,}569) for each completed model.
Unaligned prediction rates ranged from 0.30\% to 0.68\%.

Per-label legacy Mistral metrics: hard-skill F1 0.730, soft-skill F1 0.587 (\texttt{metrics.json}).

\section{Wrapper and Linking Results}

The full CAG/graph safe wrapper candidate achieved exact span F1 0.671 with gate decision \texttt{accept} (\texttt{artifacts/skillllm\_v2/safe\_wrapper/full\_cag\_graph/metrics.json}).
Taxonomy linking coverage for that variant is 0.890.
The gate accepted the candidate because metrics remained above non-regression floors despite not meeting the configured improvement target for exact span F1 (gate reason string in metrics JSON).

Boundary-only and Mistral+GLiNER+taxonomy variants showed negligible or zero delta on exact span F1 relative to the anchor in wrapper error analysis.

\section{Smoke Ablation (Limited Sample)}

Smoke ablation on approximately twenty examples (\texttt{reports/skillllm\_v2/extraction\_ablation.md}) shows lower absolute F1 values (for example, 0.057 for Mistral-only variant A).
These numbers reflect the smoke sample and missing full model inference in some variants; they are \textbf{not} used for primary thesis conclusions.

\section{Downstream Matching and Recommendation}

\texttt{reports/skillllm\_v2/matching\_eval.csv} and \texttt{course\_recommendation\_eval.csv} record \texttt{NA} for nDCG@10 and P@5 because labeled evaluation sets were unavailable.
Demo outputs in \texttt{reports/skillllm\_v2/demo/} illustrate job match scores and gap lists on synthetic inputs only.

\section{Efficiency Observations}

Legacy benchmark logs report Mistral average latency 2.03\,s per example and total runtime 784.9 minutes for the three-epoch test inference pass.
Llama 3.1 8B averaged 0.91\,s per example with lower F1.
Stage-2 training required approximately 90 hours for 80 Mistral epochs on two GPUs.

\section{Supplementary Extract\_jobDesc Results}

BERT entity F1 on the Extract\_jobDesc gold test split reached 0.462; silver-trained BERT reached 0.710 entity F1 on gold evaluation.
These results are task-specific and not directly comparable to SkillSpan span F1.
