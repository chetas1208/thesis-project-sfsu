\chapter{Analysis and Discussion}
\label{ch:discussion}

\section{Model Backbone Comparison (RQ1--RQ2)}

The legacy benchmark isolates backbone choice under equal epoch budget and shared prompts.
Mistral Small 3.1 24B leads Qwen3 14B by 0.016 exact span F1 absolute, despite higher inference latency than Llama 3.1 8B.
Capacity and instruction quality appear to outweigh the speed advantage of smaller models for boundary-sensitive extraction.

Gemma 4 26B could not be evaluated on available hardware, illustrating that benchmark completeness is constrained by deployment feasibility rather than model interest alone.

\section{Generative vs.\ Encoder Extraction (RQ3)}

Stage-2 results amplify the legacy pattern: Mistral exact span F1 (0.670) exceeds BERT (0.059) and zero-shot GLiNER (0.076) by an order of magnitude.
Generative JSON extraction with supervised LoRA fine-tuning matches the SkillSpan annotation interface more directly than token classification with post-hoc span reconstruction or zero-shot generalist NER.

GLiNER retains higher relative soft-skill F1 among non-LLM baselines (0.097 vs.\ 0.019 for BERT) but remains far below Mistral soft-skill F1 (0.604).

\section{Extended Training}

Increasing Mistral training from three to eighty epochs improved exact span F1 from 0.665 to 0.670 with train loss 0.143 at epoch 80.
Dev eval loss (0.397) suggests remaining generalization gap; further epoch scaling was not verified in artifacts.

\section{Wrapper Value (RQ4)}

Wrapper components produce modest extraction changes: full CAG/graph improves exact span F1 by 0.001 absolute over the anchor while increasing linking coverage to 0.890.
The engineering contribution is therefore primarily \emph{structured downstream intelligence without span regression}, not a large extraction breakthrough.

Wrapper error analysis identified two boundary regressions attributable to the taxonomy linker in GLiNER+taxonomy and full graph variants when comparing against anchor spans on 974 analyzed documents.
The safe wrapper gate and non-destructive edit policies were introduced explicitly to prevent such regressions from affecting reported metrics.

\section{Soft-Skill Difficulty (RQ5)}

Across legacy and Stage-2 Mistral runs, hard-skill F1 exceeds soft-skill F1 by roughly 0.12--0.13 F1 points.
Soft skills are more lexically variable and boundary-ambiguous, consistent with SkillSpan annotation challenges noted in prior work~\cite{zhang2022skillspan}.

\section{Interpretation vs.\ Evidence}

Observed metric ordering is fully supported by JSON artifacts.
Claims about downstream recommendation quality beyond demo outputs are interpretive and labeled as unverified pending labeled evaluation sets.
