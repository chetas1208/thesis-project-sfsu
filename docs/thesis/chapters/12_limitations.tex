\chapter{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{ch:limitations}

\section{Dataset Limitations}

SkillSpan sentences come from job posting domains (technical and house listings) and may not represent all resume or global labor-market language.
Train/dev/test splits are standard SkillSpan partitions; no additional cross-domain evaluation was verified in artifacts.

\section{Hardware and Model Coverage}

Gemma 4 26B experiments failed on 2$\times$24\,GB GPUs; conclusions about that model are limited to failure documentation, not performance ranking.
All LLM experiments are local open-weight runs; API-based closed models were not benchmarked.

\section{Training Protocol Differences}

Legacy five-model comparisons used three epochs; Stage-2 Mistral uses eighty epochs with different LoRA rank.
Direct numeric comparison between phases requires explicit labeling, as done in this thesis.

\section{Single-Seed Evaluation}

Saved artifacts reflect single training and inference runs without multi-seed variance estimates.
Reported differences smaller than 0.002 F1 between wrapper and anchor should be interpreted cautiously.

\section{Downstream Evaluation Gap}

Job matching nDCG@10 and course recommendation P@5 are not computed on labeled data.
Demo JSON outputs demonstrate interface behavior only.

\section{Metric Scope}

Earlier \texttt{Experiments/} benchmarks use text-level and document-set metrics incompatible with SkillSpan exact span F1.
Extract\_jobDesc uses entity F1 on a different annotation task.

\section{Formatting and Submission}

This \LaTeX{} source has not undergone SFSU Graduate Studies preliminary or final format check.
Department-approved template compliance remains the author's responsibility~\cite{sfsugrad2026thesis}.

\section{External Validity}

Results apply to English SkillSpan-style sentences and configured taxonomy aliases.
Generalization to live hiring pipelines requires additional validation not present in the repository.
