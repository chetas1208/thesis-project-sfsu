# Threats to validity

This list was compiled before the final results and its status columns were refreshed against the final layer outputs (register of integrity flags in Section G). Each threat states its evidence (a file, a decision id in
`Decisions.md`, or an integrity flag in `Progress.md`), the likely direction of bias, what this programme does about it,
and what remains open. Threats are grouped by the validity type they endanger. "Open" means the threat still limits the
claims in the thesis and has to be stated next to them.

Naming conventions fixed by the coordinator (D-C11) apply throughout. The stored `artifacts/skillllm_v2/bert_baseline`
is called the **token-lookup baseline**: it memorises the most frequent BIO tag per lower-cased training token and never
loads a transformer. `gliner_baseline` is **GLiNER zero-shot** (`urchade/gliner_medium-v2.1`, not fine-tuned on
SkillSpan). Neither is a "BERT baseline" or a trained GLiNER.

## B. Construct validity: does the metric measure the claimed property?

| # | Threat | Evidence | Likely bias | Mitigation | Status |
